## Commands

Record each executed command verbatim, including filters used for individual
targets. Repeat the Criterion targets in three fresh processes, replacing
`<round>` with `v0.5.0-r1`, `v0.5.0-r2`, and `v0.5.0-r3`.

```text
cargo bench --locked --bench graph_build -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench compile -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench runtime_sync -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench runtime_async -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench dispatch -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench async_runtime_integration -- --save-baseline <round> --warm-up-time 3 --measurement-time 10 --sample-size 100 --confidence-level 0.99 --significance-level 0.01
cargo bench --locked --bench allocations > documents/performance/v0.5.0-allocations.csv
```

For CPU attribution, use a separate Criterion profile-time invocation and
record the WPR/WPA capture details outside the timed baseline:

```text
cargo bench --locked --bench runtime_sync -- <benchmark-filter> --profile-time 30
```
