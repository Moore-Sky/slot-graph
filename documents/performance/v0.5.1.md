# v0.5.1 Performance Report

## Decision

Accepted E-01: synchronous task factories now return their result directly
instead of allocating and polling `Box::pin(std::future::ready(...))`. The
public API and observable execution contract are unchanged.

Base revision: `080733ac87fdaaa40474f5e95caa5335ba73833b`.
Candidate timings are preserved in
[v0.5.1-e01-timings.csv](v0.5.1-e01-timings.csv), with allocations in
[v0.5.1-e01-allocations.csv](v0.5.1-e01-allocations.csv).

## Results

| Reusable 100-node workload | v0.5.0 median | v0.5.1 median | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A: active empty | 11.909 us | 8.704 us | -26.9% |
| B: scalar chain, bound | 47.716 us | 35.373 us | -25.9% |
| C: fan-out/fan-in `Many` | 59.209 us | 55.492 us | -6.3% |
| D: independent Ready, inline | 29.443 us | 26.564 us | -9.8% |
| D: independent Ready, fixed pool / 1 worker | 56.035 us | 50.712 us | -9.5% |
| Local mixed 80/20 async | 54.122 us | 37.318 us | -31.0% |
| Send mixed 80/20 async | 53.501 us | 37.061 us | -30.7% |

The first B timing round was noisy and roughly neutral; rounds two and three
improved by about 26%. The acceptance decision therefore relies on the
allocation gate as well as latency. B reusable fell from 901 to 801 allocation
calls (-11.1%) and from 42,752 to 35,552 requested bytes (-16.8%). Three
allocation rounds reproduced the same A/B/C inline counts and bytes exactly.
Pure PendingOnce async allocations remained unchanged, while the 80/20 mixed
case removed exactly 80 calls.

The initially suspicious eight-worker fixed-pool result was rerun as three
interleaved base/candidate pairs. Candidate deltas were approximately -3.5%,
-0.4%, and +1.7%; the worst pair stayed within the 2% regression guard. All
Local and SendMode Ready, PendingOnce, mixed, and wake-storm variants were also
measured in three candidate rounds. No protected async workload regressed.

## Verification

- Stable all-target tests, doctests, and Clippy with warnings denied passed.
- Rust 1.71 tests, doctests, and benchmark compilation passed before the
  version-only metadata update; the final locked gate was rerun for release.
- The implementation adds no dependency and changes no public signature.
- Existing cancellation, panic, dispatch, atomic output, ordering, and stale
  wake contract tests remain enabled and pass.
